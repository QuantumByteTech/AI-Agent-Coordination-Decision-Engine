DEMO COMPANY HR POLICIES

Leave Policy:
- Employees receive 12 casual leaves per calendar year.
- Employees should apply for planned leave at least 2 working days in advance.
- Sick leave should be reported to the manager as soon as possible.
- Leave requests require approval from the reporting manager.
- Employees can check their leave balance through the HR portal.

Work From Home Policy:
- Employees may request work from home when permitted by their role and team.
- Planned work-from-home requests require manager approval.
- Employees must remain available during normal working hours.
- Employees working remotely must have a stable internet connection.
- Company-specific WFH eligibility is determined by the organization.

Attendance Policy:
- Employees are expected to follow the working hours defined by their department.
- Employees should inform their manager about unexpected absence.
- Attendance records are maintained through the company's HR system.

Onboarding:
- New employees must complete their onboarding documentation.
- Employees should complete mandatory training assigned during onboarding.
- Employees can contact HR for questions regarding payroll, benefits, documentation, and company policies.
